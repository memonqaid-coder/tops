>>Here is how you can use Chrome DevTools to change the background color 
  of your name (<h1>) live on the page:

 > Open myprofile.html in Google Chrome.

>>Right-click directly on your name (Alex Mercer) 
  and select Inspect (or press F12 / Ctrl + Shift + I on Windows, Cmd + Option + I on Mac).

>>In the Elements panel on the right side (or bottom), look at the Styles tab.

>>Click inside the element.style { } block at the top of the Styles pane.

>>Type background-color: yellow; (or substitute yellow with any hex code or 
  color name you prefer, like skyblue, #ff5722, or purple).

>>The background color behind your name will update instantly on the page without 
  modifying your saved myprofile.html file on disk.
